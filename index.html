🌍 Why This Matters

Vietnam is one of the countries most vulnerable to climate change in the world. Rising sea levels threaten coastal cities, intensified monsoon seasons cause catastrophic urban flooding, and isolated highland communities are cut off for weeks after major storms.

In September 2024, Super Typhoon Yagi — the strongest storm to hit Vietnam in 30 years — left over 300 people dead and displaced thousands of families in the north. The same year, Hanoi recorded its worst urban flooding in a decade, largely caused by blocked drainage systems overwhelmed by waste.

These games put you in the shoes of the people who respond to these crises every day — and show how individual actions, from picking up litter to clearing a drain, create real change at scale.
